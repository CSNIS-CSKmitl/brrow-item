# CS-KMITL Equipment Borrowing (`DESIGN.md`)

Inherits the root [CS-KMITL Design System](../DESIGN.md).

## Direction

- **Product**: Equipment and hardware loan catalog & request system for CS-KMITL students and staff.
- **Character**: `ordered, tactile, clear` — structured inventory catalog with instant availability and clean loan queue.
- **Moments**: The equipment catalog grid with stock availability and the slide-over loan request panel.
- **Signature**: Hairline 1px equipment cards, `tabular-nums` stock/loan counts, and a crisp status indicator.
